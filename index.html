1. Digger (2026): Power, Ambition and a Disaster No One Can Undo

Dark Comedy / Drama⭐ IMDb 7.4/102h 9m • Rated R

Where to watch: In theaters

Tom Cruise leads Digger, a darkly comic drama from director Alejandro G. Iñárritu. The film puts one of the most powerful men in the world in an impossible position: after unleashing a catastrophe with potentially devastating consequences, he tries to prove that he can still be humanity's saviour.

What makes the film stand out is its mix of high stakes and dark humour. It is not a simple hero story. It is about power, ego, and what happens when a person who is used to being in control suddenly faces a problem that cannot be fixed with money or influence. The supporting cast includes Sandra Hüller, John Goodman, Michael Stuhlbarg, Jesse Plemons and Riz Ahmed.

Why it is trending: A major director-star pairing, a bold premise, and the number-one spot on the current IMDb trending chart have made Digger the most talked-about film of the week.

2. Verity (2026): A Writer, a Manuscript, and a Truth That Should Stay Hidden

Psychological Thriller⭐ IMDb 6.0/101h 57m • Rated R

Where to watch: In theaters

Based on Colleen Hoover's bestselling novel, Verity follows struggling writer Lowen Ashleigh, who takes a job completing a successful author's book series. At first it looks like the opportunity of a lifetime. Then Lowen discovers a manuscript that reveals troubling details about the author's life, and the job turns into something far more disturbing.

The film stars Dakota Johnson, Anne Hathaway and Josh Hartnett. Its tension comes from secrets and unreliable accounts: the audience is never quite sure which version of events to believe, and that uncertainty is exactly what keeps viewers talking after the credits.

Why it is trending: A hugely popular book, a psychological mystery, and a high-profile cast make Verity a natural talking point for book lovers and thriller fans alike.

3. Unabomber (2026): The Investigation Behind One of America's Most Notorious Cases

Crime Drama⭐ IMDb 6.2/101h 37m • Rated R

Where to watch: Streaming on Netflix

Russell Crowe stars in Unabomber, a crime drama that explores the story behind one of America's most notorious criminal cases. The film traces how a Harvard student became the Unabomber, and follows the FBI investigation that tried to understand his past and stop his deadly campaign.

Directed by Janus Metz, with Shailene Woodley and Jacob Tremblay also in the cast, the film connects the investigation with the events that shaped the perpetrator. That psychological angle sets it apart from a straightforward true-crime retelling: it asks how such a path begins, not just how it ends.

Why it is trending: A notorious real case, an investigative storyline, and a cast led by Russell Crowe have pushed Unabomber to the top of streaming charts.

Survival Horror⭐ IMDb 7.6/101h 34m • Rated R

5. Heart of the Beast (2026): Survival in the Alaskan Wilderness

Action / Adventure⭐ IMDb 7.3/101h 41m • Rated PG-13

Where to watch: In theaters

Brad Pitt stars in Heart of the Beast, an action-adventure directed by David Ayer. The story follows a former Special Forces soldier and his retired combat dog as they fight to survive in the Alaskan wilderness after a plane crash.

At the centre of the film is the bond between the soldier and his dog. The wilderness itself becomes the enemy: cold, isolation and unforgiving terrain test loyalty and endurance at every step. For viewers who enjoy rugged outdoor adventures and stories about loyalty under pressure, the setup is immediately appealing.

Why it is trending: Brad Pitt, a wilderness-survival premise, and a story built around loyalty have given this film a strong hook for action and adventure fans.

6. The Odyssey (2026): Christopher Nolan Takes On Homer's Epic

Epic / Adventure⭐ IMDb 8.4/102h 52m • Rated R

Where to watch: In theaters; available to rent or buy from November 17, 2026

Christopher Nolan adapts one of the most enduring stories in Western literature with The Odyssey, based on Homer's epic poem. Matt Damon stars as Odysseus, the Greek hero trying to return home after the Trojan War, facing dangerous creatures, divine interference and extraordinary trials along the way.

The ensemble includes Anne Hathaway as Penelope, Tom Holland as Telemachus, Robert Pattinson as Antinous and Zendaya as Athena. At nearly three hours, this is a film to set aside an evening for, not one to squeeze into a quick movie night, and its large-scale filmmaking is built for the big screen.

Why it is trending: Nolan's adaptation combines a legendary story, a major ensemble cast and the highest IMDb score on this week's trending chart.
